Buddy

Buddy is a desktop helper that stays visible for notes, reminders, and meetings. In meetings it can identify who is speaking. Content stays on the local computer.

What Buddy is

Buddy appears as a small square on top of other windows. A single click opens a place to capture a note, save a task, or turn a meeting into useful notes. During a meeting it can recognize people by voice and show who is talking in real time.

There is no website login and no paid plan. There is no signup and no subscription. Notes and recordings remain on the local PC.

Who it is for

  • People who lose small todos in the middle of a busy day
  • People who finish a call and cannot recall what was decided or who said it
  • People who want meeting notes with real names, not only a wall of text
  • People who prefer notes and meetings to stay private on their own machine

What it can do

Voice identification in meetings

This is a core capability. A name is enrolled once by speaking into the microphone. In a live meeting Buddy can show who is speaking, and the finished transcript can carry those names as well. Matching is based on the sound of the voice, not on exact words that were read.

Meeting notes

A meeting can be recorded from the microphone and from computer audio. Buddy then produces a transcript, a short summary, and action items. With voices enrolled, those notes are clearer because they show who said what.

Notes and checklists

Notes can hold tasks, and large tasks can break into smaller ones. This supports checkpoint lists and daily follow ups.

Reminders

A reminder can be set on a task for later today, tomorrow, or another day. Buddy can also send a morning nudge about items that are still open.

Simply put: Buddy helps capture thoughts, keep tasks visible, and leave meetings knowing who said what, not only what was said. All of that stays on the local machine.

How it fits a normal day

  1. At startup. Buddy can open on its own, so the square is already waiting on the screen.
  2. Open the square. A small window appears with notes and meeting tools.
  3. Capture a note or task when something comes to mind. There is no need to open a large app or a browser tab for that.
  4. Before important meetings, enroll voices once. Each person speaks for a short time under their name. After that Buddy can recognize how they sound.
  5. During a meeting, press Record. Buddy listens to the microphone and to call audio from the computer. While people talk, it can show who is speaking at that moment.
  6. After recording stops, Buddy prepares a transcript with speaker names where possible, plus a summary and next steps that can become tasks.

What Buddy is not

  • It is not a social network or chat app
  • It does not upload meetings to an external company website by default
  • Notes and reminders do not require a pile of technical terms to use

About privacy

Notes, tasks, and meeting recordings live on the local PC. Optional speech and summary tools, when set up, also run on that machine. The design goal is simple: work stays with the owner of the computer.

Getting started

  1. Install Buddy on a Windows computer using the start instructions in the project.
  2. Find the light brown square on the screen and click it once.
  3. Choose New note, add a task, and set a reminder.
  4. On the Meet tab, enroll a voice, then use Test voice to confirm it.
  5. When ready, try a short meeting recording and observe who is speaking.
In one line: Buddy is a sticky note that can also take meeting minutes and identify who is talking. It stays private, stays on top, and fits a normal workday.
